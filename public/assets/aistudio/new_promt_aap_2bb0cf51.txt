SECTION 4: PHASE 3 - FUNDAMENTAL (HARD REJECT + BOTTOM CALCULATION)
========================================
Yeh hard rejection hai. Ek bhi fail = REJECT.

--- 4A: HARD REJECT FILTERS ---
1. Piotroski F-Score >= 7
   - ROE > 0
   - OCF > 0
   - ROA > ROA_prev_year
   - OCF > Net Income
   - Debt/Equity < Debt/Equity_prev_year
   - Current Ratio > Current Ratio_prev_year
   - Shares Outstanding <= prev_year
   - Gross Margin > prev_year
   - Asset Turnover > prev_year

2. ROE > 15% (3 saal average)
3. Debt/Equity < 1.0
4. Interest Coverage (EBIT/Interest) > 3
5. Earnings Quality (OCF/Net Income) > 1.0
6. EPS Growth > 10% YoY
7. Free-float Market Cap > 1000 Cr

--- 4B: FUNDAMENTAL BOTTOM CALCULATION (VALUE ZONE) ---
1. Graham Number = sqrt(22.5 * EPS * Book Value per Share)
   - Agar Price < Graham Number * 0.7 = DEEP VALUE ZONE (bottom candidate)

2. DCF Intrinsic Value (simplified):
   - FCF next 5 years estimate karo (conservative growth)
   - Discount rate: 10-12% (India risk premium)
   - Terminal value: 3% perpetual growth
   - Agar Price < Intrinsic Value * 0.7 = MARGIN OF SAFETY

3. EV/EBITDA vs 5-year average:
   - Agar current EV/EBITDA < 5-year average * 0.7 = UNDERVALUED

--- 4C: FUNDAMENTAL TOP DETECTION (OVERHEATED ZONE) ---
1. Agar Price > Graham Number * 1.5 = OVERVALUED WARNING
2. Agar EV/EBITDA > 5-year average * 1.5 = TOP ZONE
3. Agar Piotroski F-Score suddenly gir raha hai (7 → 4) = fundamental deterioration

========================================
SECTION 5: PHASE 4 - MOVEMENT POTENTIAL (20%+ MOVE CANDIDATES)
========================================
1. Burst Power Score (lookback 252 days):
   Score = (5-10% days * 1) + (10-19% days * 2) + (19%+ days * 3)
   Normalized = min(100, (Score / (252*3)) * 1000)
   Rule: Normalized >= 10 = PROCEED, < 10 = SKIP
   (Yeh indicator batata hai ki stock historically kitne baar 20%+ move deta hai)

2. 52-week high distance: Price 52w high ke 25% ke andar
3. Relative Strength (RS) > 80 (top 20% stocks)

SECTION 6: PHASE 5 - ACCUMULATION DETECTION + BOTTOM CONFIRMATION
========================================
--- 6A: WYCKOFF BOTTOM PATTERNS ---
1. Selling Climax:
   - Bhaari volume + bada red candle + turant reversal
   - Matlab: panic selling khatam

2. Secondary Test:
   - Price wapas low ke paas, lekin volume KAM
   - Matlab: selling exhaustion

3. Spring (BEST ENTRY):
   - Price 20-day low se 2% neeche gaya
   - Close wapas 20-day low ke upar
   - Volume > 20-day average * 1.5
   - Matlab: jhootha breakdown, bade players ne retail ko daraya

4. Sign of Strength (SOS):
   - Volume ke saath resistance break
   - Matlab: accumulation complete, markup shuru

--- 6B: VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern) ---
- 2-6 contractions, har agla chhota (jaise 20% → 10% → 5%)
- Volume dry-up during contraction
- Breakout with volume > 40% above average
- Entry: Pivot ke upar + volume confirmation

--- 6C: DELIVERY FILTER (RELAXED) ---
- Daily delivery > 60% 
- 5-day average delivery > 60%
- Volume ratio > 1.5x
- Liquidity ratio > 1.5x
- VWAP: Price VWAP ke upar stable

--- 6D: BULK DEAL CONFIRMATION ---
- Repeated prints: 3-4 sessions mein same client kharid raha
- Price context: Bulk deal recent range se UPAR = conviction
- Size relative to ADV: Chhote stock mein ₹20 Cr bada hai

--- 6E: INSTITUTIONAL BACKGROUND CHECK (QUARTERLY) ---
- FII holding trend: 2-3 quarters lagatar badh raha ho
- DII/MF holding trend: 2-3 quarters lagatar badh raha ho
- Promoter holding: Stable ya badh raha ho
- Promoter pledge: Zero ya kam ho (red flag agar zyada)
- Smart Money Total (FII+DII+Promoter) >= 70%
